The Multiplier Diagnostic · 20 questions · 3 minutes · instant results

Is your business ready to multiply?

Growing organically is slow. Businesses that fund, acquire, consolidate and exit multiply instead. This diagnostic measures your readiness for exponential growth across six dimensions, and shows you exactly where to start. Answer each question honestly, on a scale from "not at all" to "completely".

Do the assessmentTwenty questions, about three minutes. Free. You get your score and your weak points on screen straight away. Want help with them? Book a free 30-minute call and we take it from there.

Arrived here from Exit Ready, or not sure where to start? Either way this is the right first step. An exit is decided by the whole business, so we read all six dimensions before going deeper, and your answers on funding and exit carry extra weight in the score. If the result points to a sale, your report offers the Transaction Readiness Assessment: 24 metrics at the depth a buyer's advisers would test. And because an exit is a life event, there is a separate ten-minute Personal Exit Readiness self-assessment for you rather than the business.
