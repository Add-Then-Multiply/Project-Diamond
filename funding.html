Funding readiness, done properly

A raise goes better when the preparation is done before the first conversation. Deals reaching a venture firm through a warm introduction are thirteen times more likely to reach the investment committee, so preparation and routing are everything. The playing field is not yet level: backing female founders at the same level as their male counterparts is, by Add Then Multiply's own extrapolation, an $8.7 trillion global opportunity. This page gives every founder the tools from David B Horne's books: free.

3 · The language review: check your pitch language

Research by Dr Dana Kanze (now Assistant Professor at Georgetown University's McDonough School of Business) shows investors ask female founders prevention questions (safety, protection, stability) and male founders promotion questions (growth, vision, acquisition), and founders who answer every question in promotion language raised markedly more in her study.
