How we keep it honest

Technology does the analysis. People make the judgements.

The platform scores, tracks, drafts and assembles, so nothing is missed and nothing is slow. But the decisions that matter are made by people: the conversation about what you actually want, the honest advice when a deal is the wrong move, the chemistry between founders, the negotiation. Every judgement that matters is made by a named person, not by a model.

Where we stand. Add Then Multiply is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. When a client is raising money or buying a business, David leads that work as an officer of the client company, for and on behalf of that company. Preparing a business for sale is not, in itself, a regulated activity. The platform never arranges investments and never publishes financial promotions.
