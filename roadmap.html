Step 02 · From diagnostic to plan

Your gaps, reverse-engineered into a roadmap

Once we understand your personal and financial goals, we assess the business as it stands and reverse-engineer what needs to be true for your target to be achievable. The result is a clear picture of the gap between current reality and a ready state, and a plan that closes it.

What the engagement produces

Readiness Audit Report

A quantified baseline: your valuation drivers, the discount factors a buyer or investor would apply today, and why.

Gap Mitigation Plan

A prioritised list of the operational, legal and financial remediations required before any external engagement, sequenced by value impact.

Value Levers Matrix

Which improvements are most likely to move your multiple, shown as scenarios and ranges rather than a single number. Sector benchmarking follows once there is enough data behind it.
