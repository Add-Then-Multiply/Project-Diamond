Step 04 · The full engagement

From ready to done

When the business is ready for market, David leads the process from inside your company, as an officer of it and on its behalf, with the rigour it deserves, drawing on ATM's own relationships with buyers and investors. Deals succeed or fail long before and long after the lawyers are involved. It is managed end to end, from inside your company. Add Then Multiply is not authorised or regulated by the FCA, and exits are not a regulated activity.

The 90-day transformation arc

Days 1–30 · Discovery & audit

Full data room build. Legal and tax clean-up. Founder clarity revisited: the number, and the life after it.

Days 31–60 · Value enhancement

Margin remediation. Executive bench strengthened. The business plan a buyer will believe.

Days 61–90 · Transaction launch

Positioning materials, if the first sixty days show the business is ready: launch is conditional on the starting position and on what discovery finds. David leads buyer and investor engagement as an officer of your company. The process begins on your terms.

Before any of this, the first conversation is about you: the number, the life after it, the people who need to agree. The free Personal Exit Readiness self-assessment asks those questions before a buyer does.

What stays human

The first conversation is never about targets, it is about whether this is the right route at all, and if it is not, we say so. Chemistry between founders cannot be scored by software. Negotiation is a craft. Integration is planned from day one, because a deal that closes cleanly but integrates poorly destroys the value it was meant to create.
