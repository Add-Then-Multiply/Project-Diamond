Finance Advisory, working inside your business

Most readiness gaps are finance gaps: no clear picture of the numbers, no finance leader who has been through a transaction, no reporting a buyer or investor would trust. We start with a Financial Deep Dive to establish the true position. Then we insource what the roadmap calls for: a CFO or a whole finance function, working inside your business, not at arm's length, and behind that, the wider fractional and specialist bench below. No more than you need, and the skills stay in your business when we step back. Insourcing covers payroll, the finance function and the growth-related sales activity that feeds it.

Do the assessmentEight statements, about ten minutes. Free. You get your score and your weak points on screen straight away. Want help with them? Book a free 30-minute call and we take it from there.

Every Insourcing engagement is scoped to the business's transaction volume, complexity and the level of leadership required. Entry-level finance operations begin from £1,000 plus VAT per month, with a full finance function and CFO support priced following a short scoping review. A mobilisation and stabilisation phase (migration, clean-up, controls, transition) is quoted separately as a fixed fee.

What the scoping review looks at. Entities, bank accounts, currencies and finance systems; monthly transactions and invoice volumes; employees and payroll complexity; who owns payments, credit control and the payment run; VAT, PAYE and the external accountant's role; the quality of the current records and any clean-up needed; the close deadline and what the board needs; the team's capacity and the seniority required; and whether funding, M&A or exit work is in view. Ten minutes on the Readiness Call covers most of it.

Why insource rather than outsource?

Buyers pay for businesses that own their skills. Skills held by your team and systems installed in your business raise your value; dependencies on external providers do not. Everything we source is designed to be yours when the engagement ends.
