## 2. New candidates (Task 1 — discover)

### GIN (Gay Investor Network)

- **Type:** angel_syndicate
- **Website:** thegin.uk (JS-rendered site did not load via direct fetch this session — verified instead via two independent UKBAA posts; a direct browser check of the site itself is still outstanding)
- **Stage focus:** Early-stage/angel, sector-agnostic
- **Cheque size:** Syndicate participation from £1,000 per deal per member; no stated per-deal minimum beyond that; first live deal in progress is a $100,000 placement into a US infrastructure-tech business
- **Sectors:** All sectors — "deal flow spanning all sectors and founder backgrounds"
- **Geography:** UK
- **Female-founder mandate:** None — this is an LGBTQ+/founder-diversity mandate, not gender-specific. In their own words (via UKBAA): the UK's first UKBAA-accredited angel syndicate for LGBTQ+ investors, citing that "14% of angel investors are women, and around 11% come from ethnic minority backgrounds," aiming to diversify both who invests and who gets backed.
- **Key people:** Mark James (Syndicate Lead) — via LinkedIn, not the primary sources
- **Best route in:** Two education/onboarding routes named by UKBAA — "Pitching 101" (investor-readiness session) and "Six" (a free six-month investor-education programme run with Diversity X Ventures), leading to syndicate membership
- **Sources fetched:** https://ukbaa.org.uk/blog/2026/04/08/gay-investor-network-joins-ukbaa-as-the-uks-first-accredited-lgbtq-angel-syndicate/ ; https://ukbaa.org.uk/blog/2026/06/30/the-gin-opens-angel-investing-to-lgbtq-and-diverse-communities-with-free-investment-education/ (both UKBAA, the accrediting trade body, independent of GIN itself)

- **Stage focus:** Pre-seed and seed

- **Sources fetched:** https://tech.eu/2026/09/21/project-ventures-launches-ps5m-fund-for-imperial-rooted-deeptech-and-ai-startups ; https://www.theproject.vc/

### Tapestry VC — new London office / Fund III

- **Type:** vc
- **Website:** https://tapestry.vc
- **Stage focus:** Pre-seed through Series A, weighted to seed
- **Cheque size:** Up to $3m to lead/co-lead (grown from $500k in Fund I to $1m in Fund II)
- **Sectors:** Sector-agnostic — software, AI, cybersecurity, fintech, autonomy, deep tech, consumer hardware
- **Geography:** Previously San Francisco-based; now opening its **first London office**, splitting partner time across London, Dublin and Lisbon; portfolio spans UK/Ireland/Nordics/US
- **Female-founder mandate:** None stated; thesis emphasises repeat founders and founders from immigrant backgrounds, not gender
- **Key people:** Patrick Murphy (Managing Partner/founder, 2018, relocating SF→London); Audrey Miller (Partner, London-based); David Kelly (Partner); Robert Dobie (VP Finance & Ops, ex-Seedcamp)
- **Best route in:** Contact form on tapestry.vc ("Interested in partnering with us?")
- **Context:** Fund III closed at $80m (July 2026), nearly 3x Fund II; the British Business Bank committed $40m as a new investor in this fund. Prior portfolio includes Nothing, Hopin, and Fin (sold to Salesforce for $3.6bn). Reported here per the brief's "new UK office of an international fund" criterion — Tapestry VC itself is a pre-existing (2018) firm, not a brand-new manager.
- **Sources fetched:** https://tech.eu/2026/07/01/nothing-backer-tapestry-vc-closes-80m-fund-opens-london-office/ ; https://tapestry.vc

**Ruled out / excluded:** Arāya Sie Fund (already identified as a near-miss duplicate of the existing Sie Ventures entry in `scan-2026-09-21.md` — not re-reported); Women Backing Women Fund / Bootstrap 4F's latest LP commitments (Evertrue Capital, Seedcamp VII, Seedcamp Nation II — none are new distinct entities beyond what's already surfaced or on file); Molten Ventures Growth Fund I (Molten Ventures already on `uk_master` — new vehicle from an existing entry, not a new organisation); Angel Academe's second EIS fund (same existing register entry, new vehicle only); British Business Bank's South East Investment Fund and Investor Pathways programme (BBB and its cornerstone-commitment vehicles are not themselves new independent investors); the previously-reported Lansdowne Partners UK venture fund (not re-reported, still pending advisor promotion).
