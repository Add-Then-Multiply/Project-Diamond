# Investor register scan — 2026-09-14

Weekly scan of the ATM investor universe (`investor_mandates`: `female_investors_uk` + `uk_master` registers), cross-checked against both migration files and the two prior reports (`scan-2026-08-31.md`, `scan-2026-09-07.md`).

## 1. Executive summary

- **New candidates: three microfunds from the British Business Bank's £90m "Investor Pathways Capital" first cohort** — Openseed VC (Maria Rotilu), Common Ventures (David Houghton / Ryan Procter), and Blue Lake VC (David Gilgur / Lyubov Guk). All three are genuinely new, first-time UK pre-seed/seed funds, government-cornerstoned in June 2026, none previously on our register or in a prior report. The cohort as a whole (10 funds) is 57% female-led at GP level and 43% ethnic-minority-led — notable diversity signal even though none of the three we verified has an explicit *female-founder* investment mandate (their mandates are founder-access ones: state-educated/immigrant backgrounds). The other seven cohort funds (Evertrue Capital — already reported 31 Aug and still pending promotion; Almanac Ventures; The Tech Bros Fund; Future Impact Ventures; Firstdoor VC; Mustard Seed Fund; Twin Track Ventures — **already on `uk_master`**) were checked and excluded as not-new or already covered.
- **Verification flag — act on this: Voulez Capital** (`female_investors_uk`, "Europe's first VC for female founders") is **defunct**. Its operating vehicle, Voulez Capital Partners LLP, dissolved at Companies House in October 2021; a second related entity, Voulez Capital Club LLP, dissolved as recently as 7 July 2026. The live website (voulez-capital.com) still describes the firm in the present tense but shows no activity or press newer than 2019–2020. Recommend marking `archived`.
- **Verification flag — lower urgency: Merian Ventures** (`uk_master`, women-led deep-tech VC) shows no recorded investment activity in 2025 or 2026 per third-party trackers, and its website, while live, has no dated news. Not confirmed closed, but recommend downgrading to `verify_before_approach` pending a direct check.
- Six of eight verification picks (Pink Salt Ventures, Lifted Ventures, Guillaume Pousaz / Zinal Growth, UK Sovereign AI, Seedrs/Republic Europe, Peter Jones Enterprises Ltd) confirmed active and materially unchanged; one minor detail gap noted for Pink Salt Ventures (no website on file despite one existing).
- No further genuinely new investor vehicles turned up beyond the BBB cohort this week — kept the new-candidate list to the three most independently verifiable rather than padding with press-release-only names.

## 2. New candidates (Task 1 — discover)

All three below are first-time funds in the British Business Bank's inaugural **Investor Pathways Capital / Microfunds** cohort (announced 25 June 2026, £90m committed as cornerstone investor across 10 funds of £10m–£20m each, targeting pre-seed/seed). Checked against both migration files and both prior scan reports — none present.

### Openseed VC
- **Type:** vc
- **Website:** https://www.openseed.vc
- **Stage focus:** Pre-institutional / angel stage (before formal VC rounds)
- **Cheque size:** Up to $150,000
- **Sectors:** "Future of business" (B2B software, AI, fintech), future of work (productivity), future of health (digital health)
- **Geography:** UK-headquartered (London); also backs operators in Africa and Europe
- **Female-founder mandate:** None stated as an explicit criterion; the fund itself is led by Maria Rotilu, described in coverage as "Europe's first Black solo GP" — a notable diversity data point on the manager side, not a founder-facing mandate
- **Best route in:** "Apply" button on site → Typeform at openseed.vc/apply; PR/sponsorship only at contact@openseedvc.com
- **Context:** Founded 2024 by Maria Rotilu (ex-Uber, ex-Branch operator; co-led Octopus Ventures' £10m First Cheque Fund; previously ran the Oxford Seed Fund). Fund closed at $10m. One of 10 funds cornerstoned by the British Business Bank's first Investor Pathways Capital commitment (up to £90m across the cohort, announced 25 June 2026).
- **Sources fetched:** https://www.openseed.vc/ ; https://www.uktech.news/funding/british-business-bank-commits-90m-for-next-generation-of-vc-investors-20260625 ; https://techfundingnews.com/british-business-bank-90m-microfunds/

### Common Ventures
- **Type:** vc
- **Website:** https://common.ventures
- **Stage focus:** Pre-seed
- **Cheque size:** Not disclosed
- **Sectors:** Sector-agnostic
- **Geography:** UK
- **Female-founder mandate:** None — the fund's explicit mandate is founder *class/education* background: state-educated and immigrant founders (research cited: state-educated founders fall from 40% of stealth-stage founders to 25% at pre-seed, despite being 42% of Series C founders)
- **Best route in:** "Common Path" — a four-sprint startup-readiness programme; founders assessed "not on your school or degree, but on your drive, resourcefulness, empathy and ability to cope with ambiguity"
- **Context:** Co-founded by David Houghton (Leeds state-school graduate, pre-seed investor at Antler) and Ryan Procter (Manchester comprehensive graduate, 12+ years in startups); formerly known as Social Mobility Ventures. One of the 10 BBB-backed microfunds.
- **Sources fetched:** https://common.ventures/ ; https://www.uktech.news/diversity/common-ventures-backs-lower-income-graduates-for-startup-careers-20260702 ; https://www.uktech.news/funding/british-business-bank-commits-90m-for-next-generation-of-vc-investors-20260625

### Blue Lake VC
- **Type:** vc
- **Website:** https://www.bluelakevc.com
- **Stage focus:** Pre-seed / first institutional cheque
- **Cheque size:** Not disclosed on site
- **Sectors:** B2B software (fintech, healthtech, AI/dev tools, real estate tech, climate tech represented in portfolio)
- **Geography:** UK (London-based), backing immigrant founders building global companies
- **Female-founder mandate:** None — explicit mandate is immigrant founders ("a self-selecting group that achieves outsized success and is frequently overlooked by investors at early stages," per proprietary survey of 1,200+ immigrant founders)

One of the 10 BBB-backed microfunds.
- **Sources fetched:** https://www.bluelakevc.com/ ; https://tech.eu/2026/06/25/blue-lake-vc-closes-in-on-first-fund-with-british-business-bank-backing-for-immigrant-led-startups/ ; https://www.uktech.news/funding/british-business-bank-commits-90m-for-next-generation-of-vc-investors-20260625
