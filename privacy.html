Privacy notice · the founder platform

What we collect, when, and what we never do

This site is built so that you can use almost all of it without telling us who you are. This notice sets out the few points where that changes, in plain English.

Who we are. Add Then Multiply Limited, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 04623437, registered office 8 Ainsdale Road, London, W5 1JX. We are the data controller for the information described here. Questions and requests: dbh@addthenmultiply.com.

What this site never does

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels, and nothing stored on your device. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No profiling of visitors and no tracking across pages or visits.
  • We never sell personal data, and we never share your details or your assessment results with any investor, buyer or other third party for marketing.

If you arrive from a campaign link, the campaign source in that link is read and attached to the "Book a call" link on the page you are on. It is not stored anywhere, so it lasts only for that page.

The free self-assessments

The Pitching, Commercial Acceleration and Finance Advisory self-assessments are scored entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter leaves the page. The written report exists only on your screen and in anything you choose to print or copy. If you click through to book a call from the result, the assessment code, your score and your band travel in the booking link so the call can be prepared; your name and contact details do not.

The four scored assessments

The Multiplier Diagnostic, the Transaction Readiness Assessment, the Funding Scorecard and the Ethical Acquisitions Scorecard show your full report without asking who you are. Under the report is one button, "Send me my report". If you use it you give us your first name, work email, company, your role (on the Ethical Acquisitions Scorecard, the description of your position that you chose in the first question) and your turnover band, and you tick a consent box. Only then do we store anything:

  • your answers, your scores, your band, the assessment version and the report itself as you saw it;
  • the details you gave, the fact and time of your consent, and the page the report came from.

The score and the report are produced automatically from your answers. They indicate readiness; they are not financial or investment advice, and no decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you is made by software. What happens next is always a recommendation made by a named person, on a call with you.

Booking a call

Calls are booked through GoHighLevel, our scheduling provider. The booking form collects the details it needs for the call under its own consent wording. The link that takes you there carries at most the assessment code, your score, your band and the campaign source; it never carries your name, email or any identifier.

Clients and the paid workspace

If you become a client, the information you provide inside the workspace (your intake, the documents you upload, the notes your adviser keeps) is processed under your engagement letter, which sets out the purposes, the safeguards and how long we keep it. Sign-in uses a one-time link sent to your email address; no password is stored. Where analysis inside a paid engagement uses an AI service, the request is made from our servers, the output is logged and reviewed by a named adviser, and nothing customer-facing is issued without that person's approval.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, ask for it in a portable form, and withdraw consent at any time. There is no fee. Email dbh@addthenmultiply.com and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
