The F, A and C of FACE · buy-and-build, done properly

Growth you don't have to wait for

Organic growth compounds slowly. Acquiring (the right target, funded the right way, integrated from day one) is how small businesses think like big businesses and achieve exponential growth. It is also where fortunes are lost by the unprepared. Our first conversation is never about targets: it is about whether acquisition is the right route at all. If it is not, we say so.

The whole route

  1. Why buy rather than buildIs buying genuinely faster and smarter than building? We test the case before anything else, including against the honest answer "no".
  2. Target identificationYou often already know who. We build the longlist, score the shortlist, and protect the relationships while we do it.
  3. The human approachA personal conversation, not a formal process. Chemistry is everything, and over-engineering the opening kills it.
  4. Founder to founderBefore any offer, the founders meet. Is this someone I want to do business with? That question is answered in person, never in a document.
  5. Heads of terms and due diligenceHeads of terms set out the agreed shape of the deal before the lawyers draft it; due diligence is the buyer's verification of everything the seller has said. Transparency protects the deal and the relationship. We manage the advisors and keep legal costs from running away.
  6. SPA & completionThe agreement negotiated, signed and completed, with integration already planned.
  7. IntegrationThe C in FACE: Consolidation. Planned from the beginning, not bolted on, because a deal that integrates poorly destroys the value it was meant to create.
