Levelling the playing field

The network exists to make the best preparation available to every ambitious founder, explicitly including the female founders the funding market persistently underserves. Backing female founders at the same level as male founders is, by Add Then Multiply's own extrapolation, an $8.7 trillion global opportunity. Our funding resources are free to every founder for exactly that reason.
